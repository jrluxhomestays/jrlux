House Rules

Our rules are designed to protect your comfort and ensure the next guests enjoy the same quality experience. We appreciate your cooperation.

1
Self-Service Homestay — This is a self-catering serviced apartment. Daily housekeeping is not provided. All cleaning supplies, laundry detergent, vacuum cleaner, mop, and kitchen cleaning products are fully stocked for your use throughout your stay.
2
Maximum Guests — Maximum 4 guests per booking. Unregistered visitors are not permitted to stay overnight. Guests not declared during booking are not allowed.
3
No Smoking — Strictly no smoking inside the unit or on the balcony. This includes e-cigarettes and vaping. Violation will result in forfeiture of your security deposit.
4
No Parties or Events — This is a residential building. No parties, loud music, or gatherings beyond registered guests. Quiet hours are 10:00 PM – 8:00 AM daily.
5
No Pets — Pets of any kind are not permitted inside the unit or building premises.
6
Respect the Property — Please treat the unit and all furnishings with care. Any damage beyond normal wear will be charged accordingly. Report any issues to the host immediately.
7
Access Cards & Keys — Please keep all access cards and keys safe. Lost cards will be charged at replacement cost. Return all cards upon checkout.
8
Check-In / Check-Out — Check-in: 1:00 PM. Check-out: 1:00 PM. Full 24-hour stay guaranteed. Early check-in and late checkout subject to availability — please enquire with the host in advance.
9
Cleaning Fee & Policy — A one-time cleaning fee of RM100 applies per booking (included in your booking). Guests who do not complete checkout tasks may be subject to an additional charge of RM150.
10
Electricity & Utilities — Please switch off all air conditioning, lights, and appliances when leaving the unit. As a commercial-titled property, electricity costs are higher — your mindful use is appreciated.
Before you leave

Checkout Checklist

Checkout is at 1:00 PM. Please complete the following steps before departing. All supplies are provided — no need to purchase anything.

Launder all used towels and bath mats — place in washing machine, add laundry detergent (provided), run a full wash cycle.
Hang washed towels on the drying rack — spread them out to air dry before you leave.
Wash all used dishes, pots, and utensils — use dish soap provided. Leave on drying rack.
Dispose of all rubbish — place all waste in the rubbish bags provided, then bring them to the refuse room on your floor and dispose in the waste bin there. Do not leave rubbish inside the apartment.
Return all access cards and keys — place them in the designated tray near the entrance. Contact host for return instructions.
Switch off all air conditioning units — check all bedrooms and the living hall.
Switch off all lights — including bathrooms, kitchen, and any night lights.
Lock the front door — ensure the door is fully locked when you leave.
Send your host a checkout message — a quick message letting us know you've departed is greatly appreciated.
Thank you for completing these simple steps. Your participation keeps our homestay running smoothly and helps us maintain our consistently 5-star standard. Safe travels — and we hope to welcome you back soon.
